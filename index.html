Privacy Policy

Handlit Arcade by AuraFarming

Effective: 23 October 2026

Contact: rsk.shrey@gmail.com

The Short Version

Handlit Arcade does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no online features, and it does not connect to the internet.

The Camera

The games are played by holding your hands up to your device's camera. The camera is used only for that, and only while the app is open:

  • Each camera picture is looked at on your device, in memory, to find where your hands are, and is then thrown away.
  • Camera pictures, video and hand positions are never saved, never sent anywhere and never shared with anyone.
  • The app does not recognise faces or people, and it does not create any biometric template or identifier. It only finds the shape of a hand in the moment, the same way for every player.
  • The small camera view on screen is shown to you only, so you can see your hands. It is not recorded.
  • Android asks you for permission before the camera is used. You can turn the permission off at any time in your device's settings; the games then cannot be played by hand.

What Stays On Your Device

The app keeps a few things in its own storage on your device so the games remember them: your settings (sound, graphics quality, controls), your best scores and race times, and your best lap's replay ("ghost"). Scores are kept under the name "YOU"; the app never asks for a name. None of this leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device's settings.

Children

Handlit Arcade is suitable for players of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). It has no chat, no sharing, no links out of the app and no advertising. Parents and guardians can contact us at rsk.shrey@gmail.com with any question.

Third Parties

No third party receives any information from the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. Google Play, from which you downloaded the app, has its own privacy policy for your Google account and downloads; we do not receive any of that information.

Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Union and United Kingdom), the CCPA (California) and COPPA (United States) give you rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; everything the app keeps is on your device and under your control, as described above. You can still contact us at any time at rsk.shrey@gmail.com.

Security

The app has no servers and sends nothing over the internet, so there is no copy of your information anywhere but on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on this page, with a new effective date. A change that would collect any information would only come with a new app version that asks first.
