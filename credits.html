Voices

Alien voices are synthesized with a Piper TTS voice trained on LibriTTS-R (Koizumi et al., Google), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0; openslr.org/141). The clips were pitch-shifted and processed (ring modulation, echo) for the alien effect.

Race announcer lines were synthesized with the Piper TTS voice "en_US-joe-medium" (dataset released under CC0 1.0).
